How to interpret this score

Score = 50% decision accuracy (each triage, dispatch and assessment decision judged against hidden ground truth) + 30% safety invariants + 20% on-plan arrivals (truck arrives within its slot window or early). Weights are renormalized if no truck arrived. This is a rubric metric for one seeded run, not a calibrated probability or production-readiness rating.

Driver and facility messages are written by the LLM role-playing personas from deterministic briefs; a persona message that drops a required fact is replaced by a template (2 times).
